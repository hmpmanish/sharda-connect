# User Guide 👤

Welcome to Sharda Connect!

## 1. Anonymous Messaging
- Go to your **Profile** to find your unique Secret Link.
- Share this link on your Instagram story or WhatsApp status.
- Anyone can click the link and send you a message completely anonymously.
- Check your **Secret Inbox** to read and favorite the messages.

## 2. Direct Messaging & Connections
Want to talk to people directly?
- Go to the **Find Students** tab to search for classmates by Name, Course, or Branch.
- Click the `+` icon to send a connection request.
- Once they accept it in their **Connections** tab, you can start chatting!
- Head to the **Direct Chat** tab to send real-time messages.

## 3. Reporting Abuse
If you receive an inappropriate message (either anonymously or via Direct Chat):
- Click the warning/flag icon next to the message.
- Submit a report.
- Our admin team will review the message and take appropriate action against the sender.
